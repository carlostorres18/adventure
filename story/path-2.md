## path-2
The cliff trail is narrow, and the wind shoves at you like it wants you gone. Far below, waves smash white against the rocks.

Something about the spray bothers you. It takes a moment to see it: the drops are falling *up*.

Halfway along, a dark cave opens into the cliff.

[Look over the edge](#cliff-edge)
[Step into the cave](#cave)
[Go back to the crossroads](#crossroads)

## cliff-edge
You lean out for a better look. The wind slams into you, hard, almost like hands on your chest, pushing you back.

You lean out farther anyway.

The ground at the edge gives way. You slide, bounce, and land with a splash in a deep pool between the rocks: soaked, furious, and completely unhurt.

By the time you've climbed out, the night is over. Up on the trail, in the mud, you spot two sets of footprints at the edge. One set is yours. The other is the same size, the same boots, and walking *away*.

THE END

## cave
A few steps in, the daylight gives up. Water drips somewhere in the dark. You listen closer and realize the drips are going the wrong way, landing *on the ceiling*.

Deeper in, something that might be wings. And something that might be singing.

It's far too dark to go further without a light.

[Light your lantern and go deeper](#deep-cave) if lantern
[Back out into the wind](#path-2)

## deep-cave
Your lantern shows painted walls: ships, waves, and a tall tower with a light at the top. Hundreds of gulls watch you from their nests, humming low in their throats. That's the singing.

In the biggest nest sits a gull wearing a tiny golden crown. She looks at you. You look at her.

[Bow to the Gull Queen](#gull-queen)
[Study the paintings](#cliff-murals)
[Back away slowly](#path-2)

## cliff-murals
The paintings tell a story, panel by panel. A ferry. A dock. A keeper with a brass lantern. A village where every clock says 9:47.

The keeper has your face.

You follow the panels along the wall. The last one shows the keeper standing right here, in this cave, holding up a lantern to a painting.

You touch it. The paint is still wet. A brush lies on the floor beside it, still dripping.

Someone painted tonight before it happened.

[Wind the stopped watch](#cliff-inverted) if stopped watch
[Follow the voice echoing from deeper in](#cliff-echo)
[Back away from the wall](#deep-cave)

## cliff-inverted
You turn the little crown on the watch. The hands shudder, then start to move. *Backward.*

The cave changes around you. The drips fall down from the ceiling and leap back into the puddles. The gulls fly backward into their nests. The paint on the wall pulls itself back into the brush.

You walk forward through a world that is rewinding. Out of the cave, along the trail, the spray falling down where it belongs, all the way to the lighthouse. At 9:46 its door is still standing wide open, the way the last keeper left it. Inside, the great lamp is still burning, just starting to flicker.

The watch says **9:46**.

You climb the stairs two at a time and turn the valve before the flame can die. The beam sweeps out over the water. Far off, a ship sounds two long blasts. *Thank you.*

From the lamp room you can see the dock. The ferry is just pulling in, and a new keeper is stepping off it, looking up at a lighthouse that, this time, is lit.

THE END

## cliff-echo
Deeper in, the cave narrows to a crack in the rock. Through it you can see the cliff trail outside.

Someone is walking along it. Same coat. Same boots. They stop at the edge and lean out to look over.

It's you. Earlier tonight. About to fall.

You call out, but no sound reaches them. The only thing that gets through the crack is the wind.

[Push the wind at them with everything you've got](#cliff-ghost)
[Let it happen. You survived it, didn't you?](#cliff-edge)

## cliff-ghost
You throw yourself against the crack. The air rushes through it, out across the trail, and slams into the figure at the edge like hands on their chest.

They stumble back from the edge. They frown, look around, and keep walking.

Now you understand the very first thing you felt on this trail: the wind, shoving at you like it wanted you gone. It never wanted you gone. It wanted you *safe*.

It was you, the whole time.

THE END

## gull-queen
The Gull Queen nods once. Up close, you see that her golden crown isn't a crown at all. It's a broken watch face, stopped at 9:47.

Every gull in the cave rises together and pours out into the night.

They circle the dark tower, a thousand white wings shining in the fog, so bright that the ship's captain sees them and steers clear of the rocks.

Nobody in the village will ever believe you. But the next morning, every clock in the village has started ticking again.

THE END
